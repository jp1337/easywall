Port syntax

A single port is 443. A contiguous range is 8000:9000, written with a colon.

Every rule here applies to all interfaces. To restrict a source address, use the whitelist or a custom rule.

SSH protection

Marking a rule applies the SSH brute-force limiter to it, so repeated connection attempts from one address get rate-limited.

The limiter itself is switched on under options. Without it, the mark has no effect.
